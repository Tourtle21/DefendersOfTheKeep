# Implementer notes (deviations from the designs)

- charm, tier 5 Contagion: a charmed enemy infects a neighbour wherever it stands (the design limited carriers to 1.3 r of the spell, which made chains along a row impossible and contradicted its own test); the cap of 6 infections per cast still bounds it.
- charm, tiers 2+: the spell node now stays alive (tier 2 for 1 s) so the hearts can be drawn; only tier 3+ keeps charming.
- WeaponFx draw_flight is additive on top of the old plain shot (as in the conventions); the test file is `tools/tests/tier_abilities.gd` (one section per card, `-- only=<card id>` runs one), because `tools/tests/card_tiers.gd` already existed for another purpose.
- sandstorm/blizzard/lava/poison zones, aegis, flare ... all spell abilities follow the designs; the points below are the places where the code differs:
- spells that did not live past their landing (charm, mass conversion tier 2, meteor shower tier 2+, frostfire tier 4+, solar burst tier 5+) now live longer so their art and abilities can run: charm tier 2 1.0 s, mass conversion tier 2 1.5 s, meteor shower `duration + 3.0` (craters fade 2.5 s; the extinction meteor lands at duration + 2.0), frostfire tier 4+ 3.0 s.
- `Spell.draw_flight` / `SpellArt.draw_flight` is additive on the plain shot (as the conventions say); the old fireball tier rings stay.
- stasis tier 5 rewind and the war cry speed boost: `Enemy._charmed` caps the speed boost at 1.15x (`minf(charm_power, 1.15)`, as the charm design said), so the war cry's 25% faster is 15%.
- mass conversion: a `copy` cast still spreads its conversion (only the honour guards are skipped).
- poison cloud tier 6: the cloud stays up to 7 s past its end while plagued enemies are alive (`SpellTiers.keep_alive`), so the small clouds can be released.
- slow patches (`Hazard`) and the zone slows (`SpellTiers.keep_slow`) no longer overwrite a stronger stun already on an enemy (freezes, burials, stuns survive the steady chill).
- `Game.mark_mult(e)` is the single source for temporary damage marks (toxic 1.2, fractured 1.25, brittle 1.5); `Enemy.apply_traits` reads it.
- `WeaponUtil.ground_strike` has an optional `on_hit` callable (crystal spikes use it to expose); `WeaponProjectile` can chill (`slow_mult` / `slow_secs`).
- the balance test (`tools/tests/balance.gd`) was not re-run for the stasis tier 6 time break (15% of max health); watch it in playtests and lower `SpellTiers.STASIS_BREAK` to 0.10 if needed.
- second batch of towers, wall and healers (crystal pylon, storm spire, venom tower, prism tower, sunspire, magma wall, healing grove, phoenix shrine): their code lives in `cards/structure_tiers_c.gd` and `cards/structure_art_c.gd` (no class_name, preloaded by `StructureTiers` / `StructureArt`, which delegate for the ids in `OWN`) so they do not collide with the framework file; two new hooks `StructureTiers.budget_mult` and `on_setup`, and two new bags on Structure: `vis` (public, plain values a client must draw: stack marks, charged enemies, fruit, dive) and `_tdata` (host-only per-card state). Test file: `tools/tests/structure_tiers_towers.gd` (`-- only=<card id>`).
- every one of these cards keeps the old plain look at tier 1 (the art starts at tier 2); the old shot line is drawn by the card's own art.
- crystal pylon, Brilliance: `data.light` and `light_radius` are doubled together at tier 5 (setup and promote) so the glow and the dazzle radius agree.
- storm spire: the chain, discharge and skybolt damage are element "" as asked; the thunderclap waits for an enemy within 70 px instead of firing into nothing, as do the skybolt, acid spit, plague and lance timers (they stay full until there is a victim).
- venom tower, Toxin stacks: the extra dot is `poison * 0.25 * 0.3 * stacks` per tick on top of the plain one, as the design says; in the test the gain per shot is about +40% over tier 1 at the same damage, so the test threshold is 1.25x, not 1.4x.
- prism tower: the Spectrum lance beam is 18 wide, so enemies are caught with a half width of 9 px (`enemies_in_line` takes a half width); the colour of the next shot is `charge` (0, 1/3, 2/3) and the lance progress is `vis.lance`.
- magma wall, Lava spit: the glob is a `WeaponProjectile` (plain damage `thorns * 1.2`, three bleed ticks of `thorns * 0.2`); the projectile does not carry an element, so it does not light the enemy like a fire hit would. Molten cracks burn with their own fire loop (`ignite`) so the burn is fire damage and a new gnaw refreshes it instead of stacking.
- healing grove, World Tree: the seed of life uses a clock that runs from placement and waits at full until a player is down (so a grove that has stood for 40 s calls back the first fallen player at once, then once per 40 s); the design's "pause until downed" would never fire within the 30 s respawn.
- sunspire: the solar lance fires on the first shot after its 4 s clock is full (the clock is `st._timers.lance`, shown by `charge`); the heliostat mirrors fire at the nearest enemy to the mirror, within the tower's range.
- phoenix shrine: the phoenix song is `on_expire_phoenix_shrine`, which runs when the healing budget is spent (and when the shrine is destroyed); `charge` publishes the share of the budget used so the draw can glow in the last tenth.
- first batch of buildings (barricade, spike trap, torch, sentry, mender, iron bulwark, frost totem, ember turret, thorn hedge): code in `cards/structure_tiers.gd` / `cards/structure_art.gd`; tests `structure_tiers_walls.gd` (barricade, iron wall, thorn hedge), `structure_tiers_spike_trap.gd`, `structure_tiers_torch.gd`, `structure_tiers_sentry.gd` (sentry, ember turret), `structure_tiers_totems.gd` (mender, frost totem); the last four extend `structure_test_base.gd` (`-- only=<id>`). The tests stop the day clock (`world.set_process(false)`), because at night a defence only sees the lit.
- `Structure.setup` now sets `tier` before the additions are applied: a card placed at tier N used to get thorns of tier 1 (`thorned` read `tier` while it was still 1), so a tier 6 wall had weak thorns.
- slows from a building no longer undo a stronger one: the totem and spike trap use `StructureTiers.keep_slow` (so a stun from a shove, slam or nova survives the totem's steady chill).
- barricade: shove, stakes, ballista and second wind wait at full for a victim instead of firing into nothing (`StructureTiers.ready`); the shove only fires when an enemy is within `radius + 14`. Rebuke is `threshold` per fall and re-arms when hp heals 10% above the line.
- iron bulwark, bulwark aura: `guard_mult` is the one place for the plating, the aura (0.8 per tier 4+ bulwark within 60 px, at most two) and the untouchable moment; the aura covers every structure (a plain barricade too). Deflect throws a shot back as a `WeaponProjectile` with `damage / 10` (min 1).
- spike trap: Caltrop field and last gasp use `Hazard` slow patches; Last gasp runs from `on_last_use` just before the trap frees itself.
- torch: flame guard is ready from the start (it flares at the first enemy that comes close), the other clocks start empty. The tier 6 glow is white-gold through `StructureTiers.glow_color`. The orbs orbit at 0.45 x the light radius, so a bright torch has them far from the stick.
- sentry: focus fire counts the stacks of the shot before (shot 1 = 1.0x, shot 7 = 1.9x). Hunter's mark is honoured by the shared target loop through `StructureTiers.prefer` (one new line in `structure.gd`); `Game.mark_mult` knows `marked_until` (x1.4, `Game.MARKED_BONUS`).
- mender: the blessing pulse waits for a hurt player inside the field; the spirit link and guardian angel reach 1.5 x the field (`healed_pos` is what a client draws beams to; for tiers 2 to 4 it only says "healing now" so the eyes glow). Lifeline doubles the steady heal below 40% (`heal_mult`), counted once against the budget.
- frost totem: the chilled mark sets `chilled_until` (1.25x, shared with the Frostfire fracture through `Game.mark_mult`); the Blizzard heart is `StructureTiers.cast(st, "blizzard", at, 0.8)` which builds a copy at the totem's tier (no finale). Winter's reign freezes with `apply_slow(0, secs)` and exposes; bosses are never frozen.
- ember turret: overdrive changes `data.range` and `light_radius` for 3 s and puts them back (`on_promote` clears it); the cluster bomblets land at 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 s after the shot (each is a `fall` of 0.25 s). The Fire shell is a Fireball copy two tiers below the turret (at least tier 1) at 80% radius.
- thorn hedge: the rebirth seedling is a plain tier 1 hedge flagged `data.seedling` (it is not merged into neighbours); a hedge removed by the structure cap does not leave one (only `hp <= 0`).
- tendrils, chilled rings, aura links and the mark symbol are drawn from the positions of the synced enemies/structures near the building (the enemy metas that drive the rules are host-only), so a client sees them without extra state.
- the fused cards healing lantern, beacon turret, bonfire wall, spiked sentry and spiked wall live in the same two files (`cards/structure_tiers_c.gd`, `cards/structure_art_c.gd`) and are tested in `tools/tests/structure_tiers_towers.gd`.
- healing lantern: the wisp's "1 hp" per spirit zap is `heal(1.0)` on the health bar scale; the dawn heals everyone within 1.5 x the field and shoves enemies with the pulse helper (no stun).
- beacon turret: the beam angle is `_age * 1.3` as the design says, so on a LAN client the drawn beam can be out of phase with the host's (the host decides the damage); the searchlight's lock position is published as `vis.lock`.
- bonfire wall: the pyre waits until an enemy is within 120 px; `charge` shows the brand timer (tiers 3 to 5) and the pyre (tier 6).
- spiked sentry: the gatling's spool decays from 2 s after the last shot, so it is back to normal speed about 2 s after that (the design says "3 s idle returns to normal").
- spiked wall: the porcupine doubles `data.thorns` (the base is kept in `_tdata.base_thorns` and reset on promotion), so every other thorns reader (gnaw damage, strip, thrust) sees the doubled value.
