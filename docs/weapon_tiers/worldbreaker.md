THE ANCIENT SYSTEM: **Tectonic Plates**: Juggernaut's plates (each blocked hit, `_stored`, max 10, orbiting gold plates) and Earthshaker's Fault Lines (the `slow` fault patches of `earthshaker.md`, max 14) combined: a released Fortress turns the plates into FAULTS (each stored plate leaves a fault ring segment) and every verb leaves faults; **Aftershock** (`earthshaker.md`) detonates all faults. Worldbreaker is the weapon that remaps the arena: faults of both kinds, plus the world-sized finale.

## What makes this feel like Ancient
It remaps the whole arena: faults, plates, drifting rings, titan form and a final world-splitting crack with a slow-patch aftermath. It is the only weapon that mixes both parents' systems (plates and faults) and its tier 6 show lasts longer than any other (12 s plus the finale). Compared with Mythic Earthshaker (faults and aftershocks) it adds the defensive plates and the screen-sized climax.

## Rules for the implementer
- `items/weapons/tiers/worldbreaker.gd` (`preload("juggernaut.gd")` and `preload("earthshaker.gd")`: call the Plate and Fault helpers with Worldbreaker constants; the Juggernaut's Colossus and the Earthshaker's World Shaker are NOT inherited: this weapon has its own transformation).
- `worldbreaker.gd` edits: `release_ability()` (super() then the existing 12 + 8 rings, add faults and the third ring/plate salvo through the module's `release(w)`), `attack()` slam counter, `ultimate()` replaces the Juggernaut's `pulse_around` at tier >= 5 as in `juggernaut.md`; stage 2 at press + 3.4 s + 0.5 s (the final quake lands at 2.2 s + 2.2 s), the transformation at 5.0 s, the breaking at 17.0 s.
- Constants: `WBK_FAULT_MAX := 14`, `WBK_THIRD_R := 150.0`, `WBK_THIRD_N := 16`, `WBK_THIRD_MULT := 0.6`, `WBK_PLATE_MULT := 0.6`, `WBK_DRIFT_PULL := 200.0`, `WBK_DRIFT_SECS := 3.0`, `WBK_TITAN_SECS := 12.0`, `WBK_TITAN_SCALE := 1.4`, `WBK_BOULDER_EVERY := 2.0`, `WBK_BOULDER_MULT := 1.0`, `WBK_BREAK_R := 160.0`, `WBK_BREAK_MULT := 2.0`, `WBK_FINISHER_EVERY := 6`.
- Aftershock helper from `earthshaker.md`; plates from `juggernaut.md` (`_stored`).
- Multiplayer: faults are `Hazard`s, plates/halo via aura. Performance: the titan phase ~30 fx/s; <= 14 hazards + 8 end patches (the end patches only after the faults erupted).
