# Voidreaver (`voidreaver`, LEGENDARY, pierce, H = 5.4; `weapon_power` is x1.5 legendary x1.5 rarity bonus)

## Today
- Attack Reap (cd 0.55): `swing(0.9)`, violet `arc` r 52 (+-58 degrees), 1.0 H, knock 90, PIERCES armor and heals you `min(3, 1.0 x enemies cut)` per swing ("drinks what it cuts"). Rift (tier 2+, cd 8): at the cursor (range 110) a `ring` r 120; after 0.4 s every enemy within 120 px takes 4.0, is pulled toward the point (push 260, 0.4 s), frozen 1.6 s, exposed 3 s, then 0.6 s later the rift collapses (`ground_strike` r 40, 8.0, stun 0.8).
- Void Step (dash, cd 4): 70 px dash, hits 2.0, a `slow` patch r 30 (factor 0.4, 3 s) along it. Event Horizon (tier 3+, cd 55): eight pulls in a row (0.42 s apart): each a `ring` r 170 hitting everything for 3.8 + 0.3 i (i = 0..7, no knock) and pulling it 200 px/s toward you for 0.3 s.
- Looks: violet rings, a curved scythe. The only weapon that heals through damage and pulls.

Palette: void violet `Color(0.60,0.30,1.0)`, deep `Color(0.35,0.15,0.65)`, abyss `Color(0.08,0.03,0.15)`, rift white `Color(0.90,0.85,1.0)`, eclipse rim `Color(0.85,0.55,1.0)`. Names: 2 Hunger, 3 Gravity, 4 Singularity, 5 Black Sun, 6 Oblivion.

THE LEGENDARY SYSTEM: **Hunger and Gravity**. *Hunger*: the Voidreaver's lifesteal becomes a visible resource: **Essence** (0 to 100, aura `state`): every enemy cut gives +3, kills +8, rift pulls +2 each; it drains 5 per second after 3 s. At 100 Essence the weapon enters **ECLIPSE** for 8 s (once per 15 s): every hit pulls, the heal doubles (still capped: <= 3 per swing x2 = 6), and a black halo hangs behind the blade. *Gravity*: everything the weapon does bends enemies toward a point: pulls, drags, collapses. Enemies being pulled are marked (`wt_void` until 1.5 s) and take +10% from your hits while pulled (`Game.MARKS["void_until"] = 1.1`, bosses ignore marks). Pulls on bosses are weak (`push` is weak on bosses: do not rely on them).
Players are never harmed; healing totals are capped (5.4 below).

## What makes this feel like Legendary
It controls space itself: pulls, wells, black holes that persist, an eclipse form and a finale where the screen goes dark and every enemy is dragged to you and crushed. Compared with the Dawnbringer, which fills the screen with light that heals, the Voidreaver subtracts: the effect is a darkening, a hunger. Compared with Ancient Witch Staff (curses), the Voidreaver bends positions, not stats.
