# Prismatic Staff (`prismstaff`, LEGENDARY band (rarity 6 by the fusion rule), pierce, light weapon (95), fusion tier 2 of Crystal Wand + Staff, H = 7.33, inherits `t1x/crystalwand.gd`)

Note: this weapon is Legendary by `Game.weapon_rarity` (Crystal Wand is Ancient, +1) but is NOT one of the three named legendaries (`LEGENDARY_WEAPONS`: no x1.5 power, it can be crafted). It uses the Legendary spectacle budget but is the "entry" Legendary: its Legendary rows are the least extreme of the band (the named three, which cannot be crafted, go beyond it).

## Today
- Attack: a fan of FIVE piercing shards (`WeaponProjectile` speed 310, `H / 5` each, life 0.8, pierce 1, knock 60, +-0.1 rad steps), cd 0.5. Prism Beam (tier 2+, cd 6): three lines (aim, +-0.28 rad), each a 150 px `line` fx (width 8, 0.4 s) hitting everything on it for 5.0, knock 120, STUN 1.4, and four ground strikes along each (r 16 at 40 to 142 px, 2.5, stun 0.3), shake 3.
- Refract (dash, from the Crystal Wand): blink toward the cursor (max 60 px) with strikes (r 22, 2.5) at both ends. Geode Storm (tier 3+): the Crystal Wand's `rain` (22 strikes over 2.4 s at the cursor, r 105, 4.4 each) and then, 0.5 s later, a crystal nova: a ring of 16 strikes at 120 px (r 22, 5.0, stun 0.6) and a great strike on you (r 90, 0.9 s, 12.0, stun 1.2); ring r 150.
- Looks: pink-violet shards and lines.

Palette (white light split): the six spectrum hues `Color.from_hsv(h, 0.75, 1.0)` for h = 0.0 (red), 0.08 (orange), 0.16 (yellow), 0.33 (green), 0.58 (blue), 0.75 (violet), white `Color(1.0,0.97,1.0)`, glass `Color(0.90,0.95,1.0)`. Names: 2 Spectrum, 3 Refraction, 4 Lens Array, 5 Rainbow Barrage, 6 White Light.

THE LEGENDARY SYSTEM: **Spectrum**. Every shard fan, beam and strike carries one of six hues in rotation (`_st.hue` increments per attack); each hue adds a status (the same on shards, beams and strikes): RED burns (3 bleed ticks 0.15 H), ORANGE shoves (push 140), YELLOW exposes 2 s, GREEN poisons (4 ticks 0.1 H) and adds Rot-free "toxic" (no marks), BLUE chills (slow 0.5, 1.5 s), VIOLET arcs (a `bolt` to the nearest enemy within 55 px, 0.5 H). The status is shown by the colour of the shard and a coloured `burst` r 8. **Refraction**: a beam or shard passing through a crystal-marked enemy (Crystal Lattice `wt_crystal`, inherited helper from `crystalwand.md`) bends: it spawns a new beam toward the nearest other enemy (60%). The whole weapon plays with colour.

## What makes this feel like Legendary
Colour is the mechanic: six hues, six statuses, beams that bend through marked enemies, orbiting prisms, and an ultimate that turns you into white light with a screen-wide closing flash. Compared with Ancient Crystal Wand (a lattice and familiars), the Prismatic Staff refracts EVERY line and statuses are mixed on the same cast. It is the least extreme Legendary because it can be crafted; it sets the minimum for the three named ones.

## Rules for the implementer
- `items/weapons/tiers/prismstaff.gd` (`preload("crystalwand.gd")` for Crystal Lattice, marks and shatter; the Wand's Familiars/Ascension are NOT inherited).
- `prismstaff.gd` edits: `attack()` shard hue/params through `extra` (colour from the hue table; `pre_attack` sets `_st.hue`), `ability()` six lines: replace the `for k in 3` loop by `for k in 6` at tier >= 2 with a per-cast `hit_count` dictionary (max 2 lines per enemy), `ultimate()` inherited then `super()`: barrage at press + 0.5 + 2.4 + 0.9 = ~3.9 s, transformation at 5.0 s, the white light at 15.0 s.
- Hue helper in the module: `hue_col(i)`, `hue_status(w, e, i)` (the six statuses; uses `WeaponUtil.bleed`, `Enemy.push`, `WeaponUtil.expose`, `apply_slow`).
- Constants: `PS_HUES := 6`, `PS_LINES := 6`, `PS_LINE_MAX_HITS := 2`, `PS_FOCUS_MULT := 1.0`, `PS_REFRACT_MULT := 0.6`, `PS_REFRACT_R := 80.0`, `PS_LENS_MULT := 0.35`, `PS_LENSES := [0, 0, 0, 2, 2, 4]`, `PS_BARRAGE_MULT := 1.0`, `PS_WHITE_SECS := 10.0`, `PS_SWEEP_EVERY := 1.5`, `PS_SWEEP_MULT := 0.5`, `PS_LENS_TICK_MULT := 0.1`, `PS_HEAL_PER_SEC := 0.01`, `PS_FINALE_MULT := 3.0`, `PS_FINISHER_EVERY := 6`, `PS_LIGHT_BONUS := [0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 60]`.
- Players are never harmed. The healing uses `p.heal(0.01 * 228.0 * Game.hp_scale())` per second (see `docs/card_tiers/_CONVENTIONS.md` 11) and is capped at +10% total (10 s).
- Multiplayer: lenses and the facet ring via aura (`n_lens`, `phase`, `hue`); everything else fx/projectiles. Performance: the transformation ~40 fx/s: beams are one fx each; at most 12 live beams.

## How to test (section `prismstaff`)
1. Spectrum: tier 2 fan: 5 projectiles with 5 different colours; a dummy hit by the red one bleeds, by the blue one `slow_mult < 1`; the beam spawns 6 `beam` kinds (tier 1: 3 `line`); no enemy takes more than 2 beam hits per cast.
2. Refraction: tier 3: a marked dummy (`wt_crystal`) in a beam: a second thin beam (width 3) appears toward a dummy 60 px away which loses ~60%; the focus point flash exists.
3. Lens Array: tier 4: aura `n_lens == 2`; each attack spawns extra projectiles at the lens positions; the dash leaves a prism that splits a passing shard into 3.
4. Rainbow Barrage: tier 5 ultimate: >= 6 `beam` kinds of width 10 at ~4 s; tier 4 none.
5. White Light: tier 6: for 10 s each attack spawns 6 beams; a sweep every ~1.5 s hits a dummy once per sweep; at ~15 s `flash` r 120 and a dummy at 100 px takes >= 22 and loses its crystals.
6. Draw smoke; Crystal Wand unaffected; player healing never exceeds +10% in the window.
