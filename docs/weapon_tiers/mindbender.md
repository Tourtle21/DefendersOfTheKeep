# Mindbender (`mindbender`, LEGENDARY, element `magic`, H = 5.0; `weapon_power` is x1.5 legendary x1.5 rarity bonus)

## Today
- Attack Mind Bolt (cd 0.5): a cyan `WeaponProjectile` (speed 240, 1.0 H, life 1.2, knock 60), `thrust(5)`. Turncoat (tier 2+, cd 11): a `ring` r 45 at the cursor (range 110), 0.3 s later every enemy in it is `charm`ed for 12 s (bosses have no `charm`: untouched).
- Mind Step (dash, cd 4): a 60 px dash; everything within 34 px of the path's midpoint is charmed for 3 s. Mass Conversion (tier 3+, cd 60): a `ring` r 120 at the cursor (range 100), 0.5 s later every enemy in r 120 is charmed for 25 s. A charmed enemy hunts the nearest enemy that is not charmed (`Enemy._charmed`), is a target for the others (`ally` group), is healed by nothing, and can carry `charm_power` (hits harder), `charm_blast` (heartbreak burst on death) and `rally_point` (walks back to a banner): all three exist already (Charm and Mass Conversion cards).
- Looks: cyan rings. The only weapon with no damage verb beyond the bolt: its power is the army it makes.

Palette: mind cyan `Color(0.55,0.90,1.0)`, violet `Color(0.55,0.40,0.95)`, banner gold `Color(1.0,0.85,0.40)`, white `Color(0.95,0.98,1.0)`, heart rose `Color(1.0,0.50,0.70)`. Names: 2 Thralls, 3 Banner, 4 Puppeteer, 5 Lieutenants, 6 Sovereign.

THE LEGENDARY SYSTEM: **Will** (an army, not a gauge). Every bolt that hits adds a Will stack on the enemy (`wt_will` 0..3, 4 s; visible as cyan `star` count = stacks, 0.5 s cadence, 8 enemies max); at 3 stacks the enemy is a **Thrall**: converted for 5 s (`charm(5.0, power)`; not bosses). Converts are your army: `Mindbender` tracks them (`_st.army`, up to 40) and its higher tiers act on the army as a whole: rally, command, promote, spread. Bosses cannot be converted: bolts only add `slow 0.8` for 1.0 s at 3 stacks ("dazed") and the army still fights them.
Players are never harmed; converts hurt only the enemies that are not converted (existing behaviour).

Caps (H = 5.0): the Mindbender's own damage is small: attack +0.6 H avg (chain 0.6 x 2 shots only; mind blast 0.5 H every 6th); ability: command surge makes converts hit 2.0 x (their own damage; the heartbreak blast 0.5 H); dash 1.0 H; ultimate additions: thought storm 7 x 8 bolts x 3.0 H spread (a target meets 2 = 6 H) + rebellion 5.0 H aoe (a pack of 10 inside takes it once each) + the army's own damage (converts hit `(2.0 + level) x power x charm_power` per 0.6 s: a lieutenant at 2.5 does ~15 H per hit to others... that is the army's budget, not the weapon's: cap the army: `MB_ARMY_CAP := 40`, spread max 12 per cast, lieutenants 3, honour guards 5). Conversion lasts 25 s (a legendary window; no longer: the card allows 25 s).

## What makes this feel like Legendary
It turns the battlefield into an army: your enemies become your soldiers, get promoted, are commanded, spread, and at the end rebel for you. The ultimate is a coronation: dome, mass conversion, honour guard, thought storm, rebellion. Compared with Ancient Soulreaper (a soul currency) the Mindbender's currency is bodies on the field; compared with the Mass Conversion SPELL it adds the weapon's own verbs (bolts that recruit, commands, a dash that turns gates).

## Rules for the implementer
- `items/weapons/tiers/mindbender.gd`; reuses the card's enemy fields: `Enemy.charm(time, power, blast)`, `charm_power`, `charm_blast`, `rally_point` (all exist; `mass_conversion.md` is implemented: see `docs/card_tiers/NOTES.md`).
- `mindbender.gd` edits: `attack()` projectile extras by tier + Will stacks via `on_hit` (the bolt's `WeaponUtil.hit` calls `on_hit(w, e, dmg)`: add a stack, convert at 3 with `e.charm(5.0, 1.3 if tier >= 2 else 1.0, 0.0)`), `_convert(center, radius, seconds)` gains a `power` and `blast` (so `ability()` passes `MB_POWER[tier]` and the heartbreak at tier 6), `ability()` second press (the weapon handles the second `use_ability` while `_st.command_until > now`: Mindbender overrides `use_ability` once: `if _st.command_until > now: command(); return`), `dash_attack()` gate, `ultimate()`: the module runs the stages.
- Army tracking: `tick` every 0.5 s rebuilds `_st.army` from group `ally` with `charm_time > 0` that were converted by this weapon (a meta `wt_owner = player id` set in `_convert` and `on_hit`).
- Guards: the card's `Guard.new(); g.setup(Game.stats_of(Game.make_entry("guards", 4, "")), pos)`, added to `get_parent()`, 5 around the banner at `pos + from_angle(i*TAU/5) * 18`; they fall as normal guards do.
- Constants: `MB_WILL_MAX := 3`, `MB_WILL_SECS := 4.0`, `MB_THRALL_SECS := [0, 5, 5, 8, 8, 8]`, `MB_POWER := [1.0, 1.3, 1.3, 1.3, 1.3, 1.3]`, `MB_BANNER_SECS := 12.0`, `MB_COMMAND_WINDOW := 3.0`, `MB_SURGE := 2.0`, `MB_SURGE_SECS := 3.0`, `MB_LIEUTENANTS := 3`, `MB_LIEUT_POWER := 2.5`, `MB_LIEUT_HEALTH := 2.0`, `MB_SPREAD_MAX := 12`, `MB_SPREAD_R := 60.0`, `MB_ARMY_CAP := 40`, `MB_GUARDS := 5`, `MB_SOVEREIGN_SECS := 15.0`, `MB_STORM_EVERY := 2.0`, `MB_STORM_BOLTS := 8`, `MB_STORM_MULT := 0.6`, `MB_REBELLION_BLAST := 1.0`, `MB_WARCRY_EVERY := 5.0`, `MB_FINISHER_EVERY := 6`.
- Bosses: never converted; Will 3 stacks only dazes (`apply_slow(0.8, 1.0)`). Check `e.has_method("charm")` and `!e.is_in_group("boss")`.
- Converts count against `MB_ARMY_CAP`: refuse new conversions above the cap (the ring simply converts the first 40 by distance).
- Multiplayer: banner/crowns/stacks as `WeaponFx`; the guards, converts and ring are existing net nodes; `rally_point`, `charm_power` are public Enemy vars (replicate). Players are never harmed.
- Performance: Sovereign peak ~35 fx/s; 8 `bolt` + 12 `flash` at most per burst; 5 guards.

## How to test (section `mindbender`)
1. Thralls: tier 2: three bolt hits on a dummy convert it (`charm_time > 0`) for ~5 s with `charm_power >= 1.3`; tier 1 never converts by bolt; the Turncoat heals a converted dummy to full.
2. Banner: tier 3: after Turncoat the dummy's `rally_point` is the ring centre and a converted dummy with nobody to fight walks toward it (>= 20 px in 2 s); the ultimate also plants it.
3. Puppeteer: tier 4: a second right click within 3 s sets `charm_power == 2.0` on converts within 220 px for ~3 s and they move toward the cursor; the bolt chains to a second dummy 40 px away.
4. Lieutenants: tier 5: after the ring the 3 toughest converts have doubled `max_health` and `charm_power == 2.5`; the ultimate spreads to an unconverted dummy at 50 px within ~1 s.
5. Sovereign: tier 6: `Engine.time_scale` dips once; 5 guards in group `guard` at ~1.0 s; a thought-storm `bolt` fx every ~2 s; at ~15 s a rebellion `flash` and a non-converted dummy near a convert takes >= 5.0; converted count never exceeds 40.
6. Draw smoke; a boss stand-in (in group `boss`, no `charm`) is never converted.
