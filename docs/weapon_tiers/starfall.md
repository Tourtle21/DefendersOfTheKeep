Palette: starlight `Color(0.85,0.92,1.0)`, gold star `Color(1.0,0.92,0.55)`, night `Color(0.08,0.10,0.30)`, violet `Color(0.55,0.45,0.95)`. Names: 3 Star Charts, 4 Constellation, 5 Meteor Rain, 6 Cosmic Dance.

THE ANCIENT SYSTEM: **Constellations**. Charges are drawn as star motes over enemies (`WeaponFx star` count 4 radius 4 every 0.5 s on up to 10 enemies). An enemy with 3+ charges is a STAR. Stars within 110 px of each other are joined by a luminous line (a `WeaponFx line` width 2 in `starlight`, alpha 0.5, re-spawned every 0.5 s, max 8 lines). An enemy crossing a line takes 0.3 H per 0.5 s (quiet, aoe) and gains 1 charge; a closed triangle (3 mutually connected stars) fires a falling star at its centre. The system makes the player choose where to charge enemies.

## What makes this feel like Ancient
It plays with space: charged enemies become stars, stars connect into lines and triangles that fire meteors, and the ultimate turns the screen into a night sky with an orrery on your head and a supernova to close. Compared with Mythic Tempest (charge and detonate), Starfall's charges create geometry (constellations) that persist and chain.
