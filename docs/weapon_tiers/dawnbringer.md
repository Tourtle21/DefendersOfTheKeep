# Dawnbringer (`dawnbringer`, LEGENDARY, fire, light weapon (130, the biggest), H = 3.9; `weapon_power` is x1.5 legendary x1.5 rarity bonus)

## Today
- Attack Radiant Slash (cd 0.4): `swing(0.7)`, gold `arc` r 46 (+-42 degrees), 1.0 H, knock 120; the weapon glows 130 and every hit lights the enemy (so it is never shrouded at night). Daybreak (tier 2+, cd 9): at the cursor (range 90) two rings (r 110, r 60), a `LightSource` r 190 for 14 s, players within 120 px heal 45, enemies in 110 px take 4.5 (knock 100), are lit 12 s and exposed 3 s.
- Sun Dash (cd 3.5): 60 px dash, hits 2.2 (knock 110), a `LightSource` r 80 for 4 s along the path. Solar Judgment (tier 3+, cd 50): a `LightSource` r 150 for 6 s, a `rain` of 24 beams of light over 3.0 s in r 130 around the cursor (range 120, 7.5 each, r 26, stun 0.5), and 1.4 s later the final judgment `ground_strike` r 70 (14.0, knock 320, stun 1.2).
- Looks: gold arcs and rings; big warm light at night. The weapon of the dawn: everything it does is LIGHT.

Palette: sunlight `Color(1.0,0.92,0.55)`, gold `Color(1.0,0.78,0.25)`, white core `Color(1.0,0.99,0.90)`, dawn rose `Color(1.0,0.60,0.45)`, halo `Color(1.0,0.95,0.70)`. Names: 2 First Light, 3 Halo, 4 Sunrise, 5 High Noon, 6 Dawn of Days.

THE LEGENDARY SYSTEM: **Radiance** (0 to 100, aura `state`). Every hit +2, every kill +6, every ally healed +3, Daybreak +30, Sun Dash +10; it decays 6 per second after 4 s. It is shown as the size of a golden corona around the blade (aura draws a halo whose radius = 10 + radiance/10 px and ray count = radiance/10). At 100 Radiance the weapon becomes **ZENITH** for 8 s (once per 15 s; Radiance drains over it): every attack is a sun-wave, every ability costs half the cooldown, and a golden sun floats over your head. Light is a resource: enemies standing in ANY of your `LightSource`s (Daybreak, dash trail) are marked "lit" and take +15% from your hits (`Game.light_up`, existing, plus `WeaponUtil.lit`).
Players are NEVER harmed: no new `burn` patches; all light damage is applied by script to enemies only.

## What makes this feel like Legendary
It changes what the battlefield is: ground becomes daylight, the transformation lifts you into a winged sun and the final stage is a cinematic sunrise that crosses the whole screen. Where Ancient weapons are set pieces within an arena, the Dawnbringer's ultimate has five beats over fifteen seconds (flash, zenith, sunrise columns, sunspears, the dawn) and the first uses a one-time slow-mo frame. Compared with Ancient Starfall (a constellation board), the Dawnbringer brings light that heals your allies as well as burns.
