# Defenders of the Keep

A roguelike tower-defense / action game (Godot 4.4+, built with 4.6), single player or LAN co-op for
up to four. Defend the keep's crystal from waves, explore six sectors (each with its own enemies,
resource and generator), cast spell cards and place buildings from the keep, fuse and awaken weapons at the
Forge, defeat each sector's boss to open the next, then beat the final boss. Every run earns Shards and
reward cards; a permanent skill tree, card collection and weapon codex carry between runs.

## Run it

    /path/to/Godot --path .

(First launch imports assets. Press **H** in game for controls, **P** to pause.)

## Co-op (LAN)

One player presses **P** then **Host co-op game**; the others press **P** and click the game in the list (or
type the host's address). `./mp_run.sh 3` tries it on one computer. Everyone must run the same version. See
`MULTIPLAYER.md` (also: how new content automatically works in multiplayer).

## Controls (summary)

- WASD move, Enter interact, E switch weapon
- Left click attack, right click ability, Shift dash attack, Space ultimate
- Hold a key to aim, release to cast (a quick tap casts at the cursor)
- Inside a tower: drag cards onto the map, click towers to reload them

## Where things are

- `DESIGN.md` the design, what is built and what is open; `MULTIPLAYER.md` the co-op rules
- `core/` autoloads: `game.gd` (run state), `sfx.gd`, `music.gd`
- `items/weapons/` weapons; `items/fusion.gd` fusion recipes; `scenery/forge/` the Forge
- `cards/`, `ui/cards/`, `ui/base/` cards, card art, keep UI
- `enemies/` enemies and bosses; `world/sector_manager.gd` the six sectors
- `tools/` art generators (card, weapon, resource art) and `tools/tests/` scripted playtests

Art, sound and music for new content are generated by scripts (see `tools/`), so they are easy
to tweak or replace with hand-made files.
